# ClientPilot

A mobile CRM app built for small service businesses — landscapers, cleaners, tutors, and anyone who manages their own clients.

## What It Does

ClientPilot replaces the spreadsheets, text messages, and phone notes that most small business owners use to keep track of their clients. It gives them one place to:

- Keep all their clients and contact info organized
- See their daily schedule at a glance
- Track who has paid and who hasn't
- Get alerted when payments are overdue
- Add notes and activity logs for each client
- Create and manage tasks tied to specific clients

## Who It's For

Solo operators and small crews (1-10 people) who run service-based businesses. The kind of people who are on the job site all day, not behind a desk.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter (Dart)
- **Target Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **Backend:** Coming soon (Firebase)
- **State Management:** setState (keeping it simple for now)
