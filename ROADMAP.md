# ClientPilot Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Core CRM (Current)

Get the basic app working and usable. This is the minimum needed to start getting real users.

- Contacts management (add, edit, search, filter)
- Daily schedule view
- Payment tracking (paid, pending, overdue)
- Task management tied to clients
- Client detail page with contact info, job history, and notes
- Activity timeline (log calls, texts, emails with notes on what was discussed)
- Service type tagging system
- Schedule options with date picker
- Basic login and signup

## Phase 2 — Ship It

Get the app in front of real users and start collecting feedback.

- Firebase backend (auth, database, storage)
- Real authentication (email/password, maybe Google sign-in)
- Push notifications for upcoming jobs and overdue payments
- App Store and Google Play submission
- Beta testing with 5-10 real users
- Settings page (business name, default services, notification preferences)
- Onboarding flow for first-time users

## Phase 3 — Financial Tracker

A simple financial visibility feature so owners can see how their money is moving without needing an accounting degree.

**Weekly Summary (plain English):**
- What changed this week — income, expenses, notable transactions
- Which jobs or services are most profitable
- Whether cash flow is improving or declining
- Any warning signs that need attention

**Budgeting View:**
- Track spending against categories the owner sets
- Simple visual breakdown of where money is going
- Alert when spending in a category is unusually high

**Data Sources (in order of priority):**
- Manual entry as a fallback (always available)
- QuickBooks integration
- Bank account connection (Plaid API)

## Phase 4 — AI Year-End Strategy

An AI-powered feature that helps owners plan smarter based on their actual numbers.

**Year-Round Tracking:**
- Number of calls made
- Appointments set
- Jobs completed
- Revenue by service type
- Client retention rate
- Any other metrics relevant to a service business

**Year-End Performance Summary:**
- Compare this year's numbers to last year
- Identify what improved and what declined
- Highlight the most and least profitable services
- Flag clients that churned and potential reasons why

**Goal Setting for Next Year:**
- Owner sets a revenue or job count goal
- AI breaks it down into daily and weekly targets
- How many calls to make per day
- How many appointments to set per week
- How many jobs to complete per month
- Based on actual conversion rates from the previous year

## Phase 5 — Scale

Features that help the app grow beyond solo operators.

- Team accounts (multiple employees under one business)
- White label option for larger companies
- Client-facing portal (clients can see their schedule and pay online)
- Invoice generation and sending
- SMS reminders to clients before appointments
- Integration with Google Calendar and Apple Calendar
- Referral program built into the app

## Target User

Small, owner-operated service business with 1-10 employees. Currently using basic tools like QuickBooks, spreadsheets, or just their phone. Doesn't have dedicated CRM or performance tracking software. Examples: landscapers, pool cleaners, pressure washers, house cleaners, handymen, painters, tutors, personal trainers.
